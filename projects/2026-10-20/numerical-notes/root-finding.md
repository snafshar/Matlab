# Root-Finding Method Assumptions

- **Bisection:** requires continuity and a bracket with opposite signs; robust, linear convergence.
- **Newton:** uses a derivative and a good initial guess; may fail when the derivative is small.
- **Secant:** avoids explicit derivatives but may diverge or divide by a small difference.

Record residual and step-size criteria. Include a maximum-iteration limit and distinguish convergence from budget exhaustion.
